 # AI Use and Learning Reflection

Answer each question clearly, specifically, and in your own words.

Your responses must demonstrate **depth of thought and actual engagement with your work**. Short, vague, or trivial answers will not receive full credit.

Do not give answers such as:

> AI helped me solve problems.

or:

> I used a loop because it worked.

Instead, explain **what you did, why you did it, and what you learned or verified**.

Whenever possible, include a **specific example from your program, your testing, or your interaction with AI**.

Each question is worth **2 points**, for a total of **10 points**.
Number your answers.

## 1. AI Use

What AI system did you use, and describe one specific way you used it while developing this program.


## 2. Boolean Understanding

Choose one Boolean expression from your program and explain exactly when it evaluates to `True` and when it evaluates to `False`.


## 3. Boundary Testing

Identify one important boundary value in your program. Explain the boundary and how you tested values on both sides of it.


## 4. Verification

Describe one AI suggestion or piece of code that you independently verified before accepting. Explain how you determined whether it was correct.


## 5. Code Ownership

Select a small section of your final program and explain what it does and why it is structured that way.
